Cat Coco

A pixel cat with pointy ears, a curling tail, and a golden security collar with a tag. Cats are watchful, independent, and universally loved. Pink nose for charm.

Sprout Coco
The Trust Seedling

A little bean with a sprout growing from its head — "trust grows here." The most wholesome option. Closed happy eyes and pink cheeks. Represents nurturing trust over time.

Cube Coco

The absolute simplest version: a perfect square with dot eyes, a line smile, stick arms, and a tiny gold antenna. Maximum clarity at any size. Closest to classic 8-bit sprites.

The Identity

An oval thumbprint shape with subtle ridgelines. Represents identity verification and biometric trust. Clean, rounded, and friendly. The "security" reads through shape alone.
