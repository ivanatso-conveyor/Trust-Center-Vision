Meet Coco

Six character variations for Conveyor's Trust Center AI companion. Each explores a different personality angle while keeping the 8-bit charm, security orientation, and approachable friendliness.

01
Shield Coco
The Trust Guardian

Body shaped as a shield — the universal symbol of security. Rounded at the top, pointed at the bottom. A glowing emblem on the chest signals active protection. Warm blush cheeks keep it friendly.

Protective Sturdy Shield Form Classic
02
Lock Coco
The Friendly Padlock

A padlock with a face! The shackle becomes a playful "hair" arc, the body is a chunky rounded square, and the keyhole doubles as a golden smile. Happy crescent eyes radiate warmth.

Playful Keyhole Smile Lock Form Chunky
03
Bot Coco
The Conveyor Robot

A mini robot with a screen face showing digital eyes, a pulsing antenna, and treads instead of feet. A conveyor belt detail runs across the torso. The most "tech" variation — signals AI intelligence.

Techy Screen Face Antenna Treads
04
Owl Coco
The Wise Watchkeeper

An owl symbolizes wisdom and vigilance — perfect for a security companion. Large expressive eyes see everything, ear tufts add personality, and a golden badge on the chest hints at credentials and achievements.

Wise Badge Chest Vigilant Owl Form
05
Key Coco
The Access Keeper

A literal key with a face! The round bow (top) is Coco's head, with eyes peeking through the keyhole opening. The shaft and teeth form the body. Sparkles float around, signaling that Coco unlocks access to trust.

Symbolic Sparkles Key Form Unlocks Trust
06
C-Block Coco
The Conveyor Original

The Conveyor "C" logo itself, brought to life! Eyes on the top curve, a smile along the inner arc, and animated dots flowing through the opening like a conveyor belt in motion. A golden cap adds charm. The most brand-native option.

Brand Native Golden Cap Animated Belt Iconic

Each variation can be extended with a full sprite sheet of emotional states (idle, thinking, working, celebrating, confused, sleeping) and contextual costumes (detective hat for searching, hard hat for building, graduation cap for educating). The final choice should balance brand recognition, emotional warmth, and instant readability at small sizes (32px favicon, 48px floating button, 120px profile).
